# What's new

Plain-English notes for players, newest first. **Every merged pull request adds an entry here** (see "Changelog" in the README).
The game shows the newest unread entries in a "What's new" pop-up the first time you log in after an update, and you can reopen
the list from the menu.

Format: `## <id> · <date> · <title>`, then one bullet per change. The id has to be unique and is what the game remembers.

## 2L · 2026-11-04 · Two new maps
- New map: Hillside Villa, a two-storey villa on a hill with a winding drive, gardens, a pool and a brick core. Attackers can start at the bottom of the drive and fight their way up.
- New map: Pinecrest Lodge, a timber lodge in the pine woods above its own car park, with stone steps, a balcony, a big stone fireplace and a woodshed.
- Both are far more detailed than the older maps: real-looking couches, beds, kitchens, shelves, lamps and rugs, plus paintings, posters, clocks, and windows with frames and curtains.
- Walls now tell you what they are. Pale panelled walls can be broken. Brick and stone walls can't.
- The land outside isn't flat any more: hills, paths, steps, low walls, fences, bushes and trees, with parked cars, vans and pickups to use as cover.
- Each map has 3 objectives, hatches, rappel points, barricade spots and its own map on the location screen. They run on the Low graphics setting too.
- Dockside House and Meridian Hotel haven't changed.

## 2K · 2026-11-04 · New shop
- The shop has been rebuilt: big cards with each gun turning in 3D, rarity glows, price tags and OWNED labels.
- A daily shop: 6 new skins every day at the same time, with a countdown. Some are skin packs that put one skin on 5 guns.
- A Limited section with special skins that are only on sale for a few days, owned on every gun.
- 50 brand-new skins for the daily shop and 3 Limited ones, with new patterns: pixel blocks, studs, racing stripes, two-tone and tiger stripes.
- The separate crates for each gun and the charm crate are now one crate holding every crate skin (on any gun) and every charm. Its odds and contents are shown before you buy. Duplicates still give back half the price.
- Skins you already own stay yours. Everything in the shop costs Money, never Polish Money.

## 2J · 2026-11-04 · Graphics settings and update notes
- New graphics presets in Settings: Low, Medium, High, Ultra, or Auto (Low on weak computers, High otherwise).
- You can also set shadows, resolution, effects, view distance and anti-aliasing one by one. Changing one switches the preset to Custom.
- Low is made for weak laptops: no shadows, 75% resolution, fewer effects and a shorter view distance.
- The FPS counter can now be shown or hidden.
- This "What's new" screen: it appears once after each update and can be opened again from the menu.

## 2I · 2026-11-03 · Chat, reports and testers
- You can chat in a match: Y for everyone, U for your team. Enter sends, Esc cancels. Both keys can be changed in Settings.
- The key for planting and disabling the defuser moved from Y to J (it can be changed too).
- You can report a player from the scoreboard (hold Tab, or press it with the mouse free) or from the end-of-match screen.
- Testers now have a black-and-white TESTER badge next to their name.
- Players can now be warned, suspended or banned by the owner. A suspended player can't log in and sees how long is left.

## 2H · 2026-11-03 · Hands
- You can now see your own arms and gloved hands holding the gun. Sleeves match your team's colour and gloves match your operator.
- Pistols are held with both hands.
- Hands animate when you reload, throw a grenade, place a gadget and melee.
- The gun sits a bit higher on the screen so your hands show.

## 2G · 2026-11-03 · Gun feel
- Every gun now has its own recoil. Rifles climb and drift, SMGs jitter, the LMG keeps climbing, shotguns and revolvers kick once, pistols snap up and reset.
- Recoil is much stronger and keeps climbing until the magazine is empty. Pull the mouse down to control it.
- New muzzle flash, smoke, shell casings and different bullet holes for wood, metal, concrete and glass.
- New gunshot sounds for every gun, with echoes outside and a duller sound indoors or through walls. Empty clicks, bolt, pump and reload sounds too.

## 2F · 2026-11-03 · Bot fixes and top bar
- Bots rappelling to a blocked window now pick another one, and bots that get stuck for 5 seconds choose a new plan.
- Attacker bots push the objective instead of sitting outside, especially when time is running out.
- Your crosshair stays tight while hanging still on a rope.
- The Aegis shield now stops every bullet from the front.
- Teammates' health bars show under their icons at the top.
